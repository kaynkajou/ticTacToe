## Phase Goal

**As a** player sharing a device, **I want to** play full games with correct wins and draws, **so that** a friend and I can compete.

## This Slice: Games End Correctly

**As a** player sharing a device, **I want** the game to recognize three in a row on any line (even on the very last move) and a full-board draw, then announce the result and stop accepting moves, **so that** every game we play has a clear, correct outcome.

<objective>
Add the outcome rules to the pure engine and wire them into the existing controller. A completed line or a full board now ends the game, the status line announces "X wins!", "O wins!" or "It's a draw" in the same polite region, and every cell locks (aria-disabled, still focusable) until the next round. A full engine unit-test matrix proves the rules, including win-on-the-9th-move.

Purpose: This delivers the core value, "plays correctly every time". It guards the single highest-risk logic bug in the phase (draw checked before win, Pitfall 1/4) with an explicit test.
Output: src/engine/rules.ts, the game-over rule in board.ts, evaluate wired into useGame.ts, src/engine/engine.test.ts and src/App.gameOver.test.tsx.
</objective>
