## Phase Goal

**As a** player sharing a device, **I want to** play full games with correct wins and draws, **so that** a friend and I can compete.

## This Slice: See the Winning Line

**As a** player sharing a device, **I want** the winning three-in-a-row crossed out with a clear strike line that I can see in any color mode, and announced as "winning" by my screen reader, **so that** everyone can tell at a glance, or by ear, how the game was won.

<objective>
When the game is won, draw a static strike line across the three winning cells for any of the 8 orientations, and append ", winning" to those cells' accessible names. The cue must never depend on color alone: it stays visible in OS forced-colors mode and in grayscale.

Purpose: GAME-06 plus decisions D-01, D-02 and the D-04 suffix. This finishes ROADMAP success criterion 2 ("winning cells are marked with a non-color cue").
Output: src/ui/strikeLine.ts, the winning-line wiring in Board, Cell and App, the strike geometry and forced-colors CSS, and tests.
</objective>
